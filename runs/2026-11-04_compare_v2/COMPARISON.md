# Building the Atlantean site: tractor loader-backhoes vs EOx trenchers

Generated by `compare_sites.py`. Garden-scale site (2 moats, 2 berms, about 100 m across), moats cut to a level waterline with 0.3 m freeboard. Screening estimates: machine data in `vehicles/`, soils in `soils.json`, assumptions in `conventional.py` and `tether_sim.py`.

- **TLB** hours are operator hours (8 h days); cost = rental with operator + fuel + delivery + pump. **EOx** hours are unattended machine hours on the tether (10 h days); cost = electricity at $0.25/kWh + $40/h labour for refusals and stalls, excluding the machine itself.
- **Fleet** columns: several identical machines working at once, one per moat first, then sharing a moat at 85%% efficiency each.
